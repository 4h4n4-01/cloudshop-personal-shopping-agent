How CloudShop works

CloudShop is an AI wrapper over a shopping backend. A central orchestrator runs four narrow agents, each with one job and a few tools, turning a plain-language request into a checked shortlist and an approval-gated purchase. The shopper stays in control at every step.

The agent workflow

Agent 1 ·

🧠 Intake

Turns your words into a structured, editable brief; marks what you said vs. what it inferred; asks clickable questions only for the gaps that matter.

structured output
👤 You edit & confirm the brief
Agent 2 ·

🔎 Stylist

A tool-using agent: searches the catalogue, checks stock in your size, and submits a shortlist with a reason for each pick. Its output is never trusted directly.

search_catalogget_productcheck_stock
- (checked by Agent 3)
Agent 3 ·

✓ Verifier

The evaluator / guardrail. Checks every pick against your brief in code, and grounds each claim in the stylist's rationale against the product record. The proposer is never the checker.

constraint checksclaim grounding
👤 You read the evidence
Agent 4 ·

🛒 Checkout

Proposes a plan, items, totals, every step, warnings, and stops. Payment runs only after you approve that exact plan (bound to a hash). A spending cap needs an explicit override.

MockPayhash-bound approval
👤 You approve before any charge

Memory sits across the loop: a returning customer's history pre-fills the brief, biases the Stylist, and surfaces what look-alike shoppers chose, evaluated offline (see the report).
